# Background music

Drop `.mp3`, `.m4a`, `.aac`, `.wav` or `.ogg` files here. They appear in the
**Audio → Background music** menu when creating clips (via `GET /music`) and
can be mixed under every clip, either a chosen track or a random one.

Only add music you have the rights to use. Royalty-free sources include the
YouTube Audio Library, Pixabay Music and Free Music Archive (check each
track's license). TikTok and Instagram trending sounds cannot be downloaded
for reuse; add those inside the app when you post.
